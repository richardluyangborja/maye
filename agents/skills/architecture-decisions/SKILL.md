# Architecture decisions

Use this skill when selecting or materially changing a technology, architectural pattern, or durable system behavior.

- Read current context, specs, and related ADRs.
- Describe context, constraints and alternatives fairly.
- Consider simplicity, operational cost, privacy, scalability, developer experience, testability and reversibility.
- Avoid architecture astronautics, new infrastructure without demonstrated need, and premature abstraction.
- Record the decision, rationale, trade-offs, consequences and follow-up.
- Clearly distinguish proposed from accepted decisions; do not self-approve when task authorization or review is required.
- Keep accepted ADRs immutable; supersede rather than rewriting history.
