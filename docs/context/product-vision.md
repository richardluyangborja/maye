# Product vision

Maye is a private digital scrapbook for two partners. It helps them preserve the everyday and meaningful parts of their relationship, revisit their shared history, and gradually build a personal archive that could one day be meaningful to their family.

Maye should feel warm, intimate, playful, and personal—more like a scrapbook than a productivity dashboard or public social platform.

## Guiding outcomes
- Preserve shared memories and media with context.
- Make it easy to revisit a relationship through time, places, and recaps.
- Encourage thoughtful, optional connection through letters and questions.
- Capture shared dreams and experiences through a bucket list.
- Keep the space private, dependable, and respectful of both partners.

## Product personality
Cozy, sincere, playful, mature-but-cute, uncluttered, and accessible. Delight should come from details and interaction—not noise or pressure.
